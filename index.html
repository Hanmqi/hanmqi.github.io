Mengqi Han

Understanding mineral systems
through spectra and data.

I study mineral deposits using mineral characterisation, reflectance spectroscopy and machine learning. My research connects laboratory measurements with quantitative models of rare earth element mineralisation and geological systems.

I completed my master's degree at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, conducting research at the Guangzhou Institute of Geochemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Research interests

MINERAL SYSTEMS

Rare earth mineralisation

Characterising REE mineralisation in weathered regolith and its parent rocks. Combining mineralogical and geochemical observations to quantify REE-bearing accessory minerals and distinguish protoliths.

Regolith · Mineral chemistry · REE
SPECTRAL CHARACTERISATION

Minerals through spectra

Using visible to shortwave-infrared reflectance spectroscopy to identify minerals, extract diagnostic spectral features and build quantitative models of mineral composition and mineralisation.

VSWIR · Feature extraction · Quantification
COMPUTATIONAL GEOSCIENCE

Learning geological patterns

Developing machine-learning methods for mineral characterisation and exploration, with experience in neural networks, clustering and multimodal 3D geological generation.

Machine learning · 3D modelling · Exploration
